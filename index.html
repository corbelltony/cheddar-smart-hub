A small hub for everyday life.

Cheddar runs on a personally managed Raspberry Pi. It provides a local calendar view, a weather forecast, and navigation to media services.

Connecting Google Calendar is optional. With your permission, Cheddar reads appointments for display on your dashboard. It cannot create, edit, or delete events.
